extract vs restore

Knowledge
extract
to remove or obtain something from something else
SOURCE → take/get something out
Pattern: extract A from B
中文：从 B 中提取 / 取出 A
restore
to return something to an earlier, normal, or good condition
LOST / DAMAGED → return to normal
Pattern: restore A to B
中文：使 A 恢复到原来 / 正常状态
点击带虚线下划线的词，可查看英文释义
